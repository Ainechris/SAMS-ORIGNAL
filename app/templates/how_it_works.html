A SIMPLE GUIDE

How SAMS works

SAMS (School Attendance Management System) helps the school manage student attendance and student records in one place.

01

Student registration

When the administrator opens registration, a new student can submit their name, level and trade. The administrator reviews the application before approving it.

02

Student records

After approval, the student is added to the school register and receives a student ID. Administrators can also add students directly.

03

Gate check-in

At the school gate, the student provides their student ID. The system records attendance when the gate session is active.

04

Student Portal

Students can sign in to view their own attendance history and account information.

05

Staff management

Authorized administrators manage student records and staff access. Security staff operate gate sessions and attendance check-in.
